No Kanji is imported automatically to your Obsidian vault or Anki. Use `:JPKanjiList` to browse and `:JPKanjiQuiz` to practise.

## Beyond 80 characters

Japanese has thousands of Kanji. The official 常用漢字 (Jōyō Kanji) list contains **2,136** commonly used characters, and personal/place names can use more. Completing the whole list at once is not a practical beginner task. Work from the 80 above toward Grade 2 and general-use Kanji, checking word-specific readings in a dictionary.

Reference: Japanese Ministry of Education (MEXT), 学年別漢字配当表 and 常用漢字表.
